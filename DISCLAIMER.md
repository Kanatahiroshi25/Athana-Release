## No content is hosted

- Athana **does not host, store, upload or distribute** any music, video, lyrics or cover art. There is no Athana server that holds them.

- Lyrics are looked up on third-party services (LRCLIB, NetEase Cloud Music) when you ask. Covers come from MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive. All of it belongs to its writers, artists, publishers and labels.
